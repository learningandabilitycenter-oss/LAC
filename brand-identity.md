# LAC + NBF Brand Identity

## Source Insight Summary

Learning & Ability Counselling Centre (LAC) is the commercial clinical enterprise led by Vineela Anney, MHI5712. Its public promise is professional psychological counselling, hypnotherapy, speech and language therapy, behaviour therapy, learning support, developmental support, family counselling, addiction counselling, graphology, career guidance, and emotional wellness support.

Neuro Bridge Foundation (NBF) is the philanthropic arm. Its role is to extend care to underprivileged and rural communities through free counselling, therapy camps, rural education support, medical and surgical assistance, mental health awareness, basic needs support, and physical donations such as stationery, ration, blankets, clothes, and toys.

The bridge between the two brands should be explained as a CSR-aligned model:

- LAC provides paid, confidential, assessment-led clinical services.
- NBF channels charitable support, membership contributions, and physical donations into free or assisted community programs.
- The shared founder story creates moral continuity, but the operating roles remain distinct.
- The public language should avoid implying government CSR registration, tax benefits, or legal statuses that are not explicitly documented.

## Brand Architecture Statement

Learning & Ability Counselling Centre is the professional clinical practice. Neuro Bridge Foundation is its social impact arm. Together, they create a care ecosystem where paid therapeutic services sustain professional clinical delivery, while charitable contributions extend access to children, families, and communities who cannot otherwise afford support.

## Voice Principles

- Clinical without being cold.
- Compassionate without sounding informal.
- Official without overstating legal or regulatory claims.
- Founder-led without making the story feel sensational.
- Outcome-oriented without promising cures.

## Identity System

### Shared Visual DNA

- Geometry: circular containment, rounded clinical lines, bridge arcs, and human-centred forms.
- Palette: clinical teal, deep teal, foundation plum, warm gold, soft aqua, and a restrained coral accent.
- Style: flat vector, minimalist, scalable, no text inside the icon, no photo effects.
- Mood: trusted, professional, human, accessible, and institution-ready.

### LAC Mark Direction

LAC should feel like a high-end mental health and developmental care facility. It can reference a calm human profile, a mind pathway, a subtle therapy spiral, and protective clinical arcs. It should not preserve the current mandala, floral detail, glitter border, rainbow dust, or circular text treatment.

### NBF Mark Direction

NBF should feel like a credible philanthropic foundation. It can reference a bridge, support hands, neural nodes, and community connection. It should not preserve the current unzipping head, cosmic library, staircase, photoreal details, heavy gold seal, or decorative border.

- Social Impact: "Be the bridge. Change a life."
- Founder Credibility: professional training shaped by lived experience, patience, and service.

## Compliance Notes

- Do not promise guaranteed cures or fixed timelines.
- Do not claim tax exemption, government recognition, or CSR certification unless documentation is supplied.
- Keep emergency disclaimers visible.
- Treat donations and clinical fees as separate channels.
- Use "support", "assist", "provide access", and "awareness" instead of claims that imply medical certainty.
